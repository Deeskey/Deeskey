Reliable Power & Starting—Without the Tow Truck

When your car won't crank, your battery light comes on, or electrical components start acting up, getting towed to a repair shop is the last thing you want to deal with. Deeskey Auto Service brings professional mobile electrical and charging system repairs directly to your driveway or workplace across Provo, Orem, Spanish Fork, Springville, Payson, Salem, Mapleton, and surrounding Utah County areas.

Electrical & Charging Services

  • Alternator Replacement & Testing: Diagnosing weak or failing alternators and installing quality replacements so your battery stays properly charged.
  • Starter Replacement: Fixing clicking, slow-cranking, or dead starters right where your vehicle sits.
  • Battery Testing & Replacement: Load-testing batteries, cleaning corroded terminals, and replacing worn-out batteries on-site.
  • Wiring, Fuses & Relays: Tracking down blown fuses, faulty relays, bad grounds, damaged connectors, and short circuits.
  • Parasitic Draw & Sensor Issues: Finding what’s draining your battery overnight and replacing faulty sensors or switches.

Common Warning Signs

Don't wait until your vehicle leaves you stranded. Reach out if you notice a glowing battery light on the dash, dimming headlights, slow engine cranking, a clicking sound when turning the key, or a battery that keeps dying after a jump start.
